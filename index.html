Ruhum Privacy Policy

AI mood journal and action coach. Last updated November 12, 2026.

The short version. Your journal lives on your phone; Ruhum has no servers and no accounts of its own. The AI features are off until you give explicit, opt-in consent and confirm you are 18 or older. With consent, your chat messages and summaries of your mood, energy and activities are sent directly to our AI provider, Anthropic, in the United States, to write replies and reports. The text of your notes is sent only if you separately allow it. You can withdraw either choice at any time in Settings → Privacy, and delete everything from the app.

1. Who is responsible

Ruhum is developed and published by Berk Çelebi, an individual developer based in Washington State, USA, who decides how the app handles personal data (the "data controller"). Contact: info@ruhum.app. App support: support@ruhum.app.

2. Data that stays on your device

Ruhum stores the following only in the app's storage on your device:

  • Check-ins: mood, energy, activity tags, notes, profile mode (professional, parent or both)
  • Chat history, AI memory (Premium), weekly reports
  • Your display name, app settings and your privacy choices (including the date you gave or withdrew consent)
  • If you sign in with Apple: the identifier, name and email Apple provides, stored on the device only

None of this is uploaded to us; we operate no servers. It may be included in your own iCloud device backup under your Apple settings. Deleting the app, or using Delete All My Data or Delete Account in Settings, removes it.

3. AI features and what is sent

The AI features are the AI chat, the weekly AI report (Premium) and AI memory (Premium). They only work after you tick the consent boxes on the "Your data, your choice" screen. The short tip shown after each check-in is written on your device and sends nothing.

With your consent, the app sends data directly from your device to Anthropic, PBC (United States), which acts as our AI service provider. Requests do not pass through any Ruhum server and carry no account or device identifier.

DataWhen it is sent
Messages you type in the AI chat (recent conversation for context)Each time you send a chat message
Mood and energy scores, activity tags, check-in dates, profile modeWeekly AI report and AI memory refresh
The text of your check-in notesOnly if you also ticked "include my notes"
Your display namePremium AI memory only, so the coach can address you

These are health-related data. Anthropic processes them to generate a response and handles API data under its own terms and privacy policy; Ruhum receives no copy. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Never sent: chat messages and check-in notes that read like thoughts of suicide or self-harm. They are recognised on your device and answered with crisis lines instead (see section 11).

4. Subscriptions, sign-in and payments

  • Apple processes all payments. We never see your card details.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status using an anonymous app user ID. No health data is shared with RevenueCat. RevenueCat privacy policy
  • Sign in with Apple is optional. Ruhum works without an account.

5. No analytics, ads or tracking

Ruhum contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, shows no ads and does not track you across apps or websites. Because we do not track, browser "Do Not Track" signals require no different handling. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Withdraw consent: Settings → Privacy → turn off AI features (stops all AI sending) or Share notes with AI. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent and does not affect the rest of the app.
  • Access and portability: everything is visible in the app; Settings → Backup exports a full JSON copy; Premium can also export CSV/PDF.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete All My Data, or Delete Account if you signed in. Premium users can wipe the AI memory with Forget me.
  • Questions or requests: email info@ruhum.app. Because we hold no copy of your data, most requests are answered by explaining the in-app controls; we respond within the time the applicable law requires (at the latest 30 days under KVKK and 45 days under Washington law).

8. US state privacy laws

Washington residents, and anyone whose consumer health data is covered by Washington's My Health My Data Act or similar laws (such as Nevada's), should read our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. It describes the health data involved, the opt-in consents, the processors we use and how to exercise and appeal your rights. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information, and the rights above are available to you.

9. Age requirement

Ruhum is for adults aged 18 or older and is rated 18+ on the App Store. You must also confirm you are 18 or older before turning on the AI features. Ruhum is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor is using the app, contact info@ruhum.app.

10. Security and breach notification

Data is kept in on-device storage protected by your device's own encryption and passcode. Communication with the AI provider uses HTTPS/TLS. Because there is no Ruhum server, there is no central database to breach. If we learn of a breach affecting your health data, for example at a service provider, we will notify affected users and regulators as required by law, including the US Federal Trade Commission's Health Breach Notification Rule and, for users in Turkey, the KVKK Board.

11. Safety and crisis support

Ruhum is a self-help wellness tool. It is not a medical device, not therapy and not a substitute for professional care, and the coach is an AI, not a person. If you are thinking about ending your life or hurting yourself, contact emergency services or a crisis line now (Türkiye 112, US and Canada 988, UK and Ireland 116 123, other countries: findahelpline.com). Our crisis protocol is published at ruhum.app.

12. Changes and contact

We will update this page, and its date, before any change takes effect. If a change affects what is sent to the AI provider, the app will ask for your consent again. Questions: info@ruhum.app.
