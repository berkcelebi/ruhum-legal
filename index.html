Ruhum Privacy Policy

Feel · Understand · Act

The short version: Everything you write is stored on your device and never on our servers — we have no servers. To generate your AI coaching and weekly report, Ruhum sends your check-in data directly to Anthropic's Claude API. By default that includes the notes you write. You can turn note sharing off at any time in Settings → Privacy, and only mood, energy and activity data will be sent.

1. Data That Stays on Your Device

Ruhum stores all personal data locally on your device. This includes:

  • Mood and energy check-ins
  • Journal notes and personal reflections
  • Activity tags and daily patterns
  • Chat conversation history
  • App preferences and settings

This data is stored using on-device storage. Ruhum operates no servers of its own, so none of it is uploaded to us or backed up by us. Deleting the app, or using Delete Account / Delete All My Data in Settings, removes it permanently. Some of this data is sent to Anthropic to generate AI responses — see section 2.

2. What We Send to AI Services

AI features (Action Coach, AI Chat, Weekly Report, AI Memory) work by sending data from your device directly to Anthropic's Claude API. The request does not pass through any Ruhum server, and it carries no account ID or device identifier.

Always sent when you use an AI feature:

  • Mood score (e.g. 4 out of 5) and energy level
  • Selected activity tags (e.g. "work", "exercise")
  • Dates of the check-ins in the period being analysed
  • Your chosen profile mode (professional / parent / both)
  • Any message you type into the AI Chat

Sent by default, and you can turn it off:

  • The notes you write in your check-ins. Your written notes are included in the prompt used to generate your weekly report and, for Premium users, your AI Memory profile. This is on by default.

To stop it, turn off Share notes with AI in Settings → Privacy (available to all users, free and Premium). With it off, your notes never leave your device and the AI works from mood, energy and activity data alone.

Sent for Premium users only:

  • Your display name, if you have one saved, so the AI coach can address you personally. It is included in the AI Memory context sent with coaching and report requests.

Anthropic processes these requests to generate a response. Ruhum does not receive, store, or have any access to a copy. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy for how they handle API data.

3. Subscription and Payment Data

Ruhum uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, which connects to Apple App Store and Google Play billing. We never see, store, or process your credit card or payment details. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple or Google.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID to manage your subscription status. No personal information is shared with RevenueCat.

4. Analytics

Ruhum currently collects no usage analytics and no crash reports. No analytics SDK is included in the app.

If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and any such data will be anonymous, unlinkable to your identity, and free of personal health content.

5. Data Security

  • Biometric Lock: Ruhum supports Face ID and Touch ID to prevent unauthorized access to your data.
  • On-Device Storage: All sensitive data remains on your device, protected by your device's built-in encryption.
  • Secure API Communication: All communication with AI services uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.

6. Your Rights

Since your data lives on your device, you have full control:

  • Access: All your data is visible in the app at any time. Premium users can additionally review exactly what the AI has stored about them under Settings → AI Memory.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete Account permanently removes your account and all data associated with it, from within the app. No request to us is needed.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Delete All My Data wipes everything and returns you to first-time setup. Deleting the app does the same.
  • Withdraw consent: Turn off Share notes with AI in Settings → Privacy to stop sending your written notes. Premium users can also erase the AI's stored profile with Forget me under AI Memory.
  • Export: Export your check-ins as CSV or PDF (Premium), or take a full JSON backup from Settings → Backup.
  • Portability: Your data is yours — we never hold a copy.

7. KVKK Compliance (Turkey)

For users in Turkey: Ruhum complies with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698). Personal data is stored only on your device and is never transferred to a Ruhum server — we operate none. When you use an AI feature, the data described in section 2 is transferred to Anthropic (United States) to generate your response; this processing is based on your explicit consent, given by using the feature, and note sharing can be withdrawn at any time in Settings → Privacy without affecting the rest of the app. You may exercise your KVKK rights, including erasure, directly in the app via Delete Account, or by contacting us at the address in section 12.

8. Children's Privacy

Ruhum is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13 years of age.

9. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic (Claude API): Receives the check-in data described in section 2 — mood, energy, activities, your chat messages, and, unless you turn it off, your written notes — to generate AI responses. Requests carry no account or device identifier. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription status with anonymous user IDs. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple / Google: Handles all payment processing through their respective app stores.

10. Mental Health Disclaimer

Ruhum is not a medical device, therapy tool, or substitute for professional mental health care. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact a qualified healthcare professional or your local emergency services.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of Ruhum after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
