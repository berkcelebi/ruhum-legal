Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Ruhum, AI mood journal and action coach. Last updated November 12, 2026.

This policy is published under Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) and applies to "consumer health data" as defined there and in similar state laws. It supplements our general Privacy Policy. Ruhum is published by Berk Çelebi, an individual developer in Washington State ("we").

1. Consumer health data we handle, and why

CategoryPurpose and use
Mood and energy scores, activity tags, check-in dates, profile modeShow your journal, charts and history on your device; with your consent, generate the weekly AI report and AI memory
Free-text check-in notesStored on your device; included in AI requests only if you separately consent
Messages in the AI chatWith your consent, generate the coach's replies
Inferences in the AI memory (Premium), such as mood trends and patternsWith your consent, personalise the coach

We do not use consumer health data for advertising, profiling for third parties, or any purpose other than providing the features you ask for. We do not use geolocation and do not geofence health care locations.

2. Sources

All of it comes from you, entered in the app. Inferences are derived on your device or by our AI processor from what you entered.

3. Consent

  • Collection: your entries are stored only on your own device to provide the journal you requested; they are not collected by us.
  • Sharing with our AI processor: nothing is sent until you give separate, opt-in consent on the "Your data, your choice" screen and confirm you are 18 or older. Sending your note text needs its own, additional consent.
  • Withdrawal: Settings → Privacy, at any time. After withdrawal no further data is sent.
  • Sale: we do not sell consumer health data, and would never do so without the signed authorization the law requires.

4. Who receives consumer health data

RecipientRoleWhat
Anthropic, PBC (San Francisco, USA)AI processor, acting on our behalf to generate responsesOnly after your consent: the data listed in section 1 for the feature you use

We have no affiliates. No consumer health data is shared with RevenueCat, Apple (beyond your own device backups) or anyone else. Messages that suggest a crisis are never sent to the AI processor.

5. Your rights

  • Access: confirm whether we handle your consumer health data and get a copy. Everything is visible in the app, and Settings → Backup exports a full copy. On request we also confirm the recipients listed above and their contact details.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete All My Data (or Delete Account) removes it from your device immediately. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further for us to delete; data already sent to Anthropic is handled under its retention terms, and on request we will ask Anthropic to delete what it can identify.
  • Withdraw consent: Settings → Privacy.
  • No discrimination: exercising these rights never changes the price or quality of the rest of the app.

To make a request, email info@ruhum.app. We respond within 45 days, extendable once by 45 days when reasonably necessary, and tell you if we do so.

6. Appeals

If we decline your request, reply to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject line. We will review it and reply in writing within 45 days, explaining our decision. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.
